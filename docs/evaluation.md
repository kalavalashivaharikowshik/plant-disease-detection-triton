## Headline numbers

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Top-1 accuracy | **94.2%** (358/380) |
| Top-3 accuracy | 100.0% (380/380) |
| Results marked reliable | 89.5% of images |

The reliability flag is the point of the app: when LeafScan says a result is reliable it is right far more often, and most mistakes arrive with a warning that tells the user to double-check.

## Caveats

- PlantVillage photos show one leaf on a plain background in controlled light. The model was trained on an augmented copy of the same dataset, so some test images may overlap with training data and these numbers are optimistic.
- Expect lower accuracy on field photos (several leaves, soil, shadows). Fine-tuning on a field dataset such as PlantDoc is the main next step.
